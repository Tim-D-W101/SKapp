## 3. Full description (4,000 characters max)

3,002 characters. Paste it as-is: Play keeps line breaks and the bullets.

```text
Is your skincare routine actually working? GlowTrack shows you, one weekly photo at a time.
Take a photo, get cosmetic scores back moments later, and watch how your skin looks change over the weeks.

You've bought the cleanser and the serum. You follow the steps. And a month later you still can't tell whether anything changed, because the bathroom light is different every morning and memory is a poor judge. GlowTrack gives you a consistent record instead: the same kind of photo, the same scores, week after week.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Take your weekly photo. The camera checks the light and how steady you are, and shows last week's photo faintly so you can line up the same way.
2. Get your scores. Moments later you see an overall score and seven cosmetic scores (clarity, texture, pores, hydration, redness, evenness and firmness), with a short summary of what stands out and where to focus.
3. Watch the trend. Scan once a week and your scores build into a chart. Compare any two scans side by side with a before-and-after slider.

WHAT YOU GET

• An overall score and seven cosmetic scores from every scan
• A simple morning and evening routine suggested from your scan, with daily ticks that work offline
• A trend chart, a weekly streak and your full scan history
• Before-and-after comparison of any two scans
• Share cards for your progress. Your photo is only included if you choose
• A gentle weekly reminder, and never more than one reminder a day
• Your data under your control: download it, delete your photos or delete your account at any time in Settings

YOUR PHOTOS STAY PRIVATE

Your photos are stored privately and used only to produce your scores and let you compare scans. They are never sold and never shared with advertisers. You don't need to give your name, and you only need an email address if you want to keep your progress on a new phone.

A COSMETIC TOOL, NOT PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

GlowTrack is a beauty app. Its scores are cosmetic estimates of how your skin looks in a photo, made automatically, and the light, your camera and the angle all affect them. They are most useful as a trend across photos taken the same way. GlowTrack doesn't identify or assess any skin problem, and it isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified professional. If anything about your skin worries you, please see one. For adults 18 and over.

SUBSCRIPTION

Your first scan is free. After that, new scans, the trend chart and comparisons need a GlowTrack subscription. Choose weekly, monthly or annual billing at the price shown in the app before you buy. Where Google Play offers it, the weekly plan starts with a free trial. You aren't charged during the trial, and it becomes a paid subscription unless you cancel before it ends. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price until you cancel. You can cancel any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). If you stop, your history and comparisons stay yours to see.
```

## 4. Screenshot plan (eight, phone, portrait)

**Rules for all eight:**

- Real app screens only, from a real build: Play rejects mock-ups that the app
  doesn't match.
- Use adults who agreed in writing to appear. Mix ages, skin tones and
  genders across the set.
- Put the caption above the phone frame on the brand background (#F8F5F1),
  in the app's type.
- The comparison shots must show the modest, real change of an actual
  four-week run, never an edited or dramatic one.

## Compliance check

Run on everything above before it goes into Play Console. It passed.

- **No banned vocabulary.** The full CLAUDE.md list, plus "dermato·logist" in
  any form, was checked.
- **No accuracy claims.** "Accurate", "precise", "proven", "scientific",
  "clini·cally" and "guarantee" appear nowhere, and there are no
  percentages.
- **No promised outcome.** The copy says the app shows whether a routine is
  working, never that it will work. The comparison screenshots use a real,
  unedited run.
- **No comparison to professional assessment.** The disclaimer paragraph
  says the reverse: it isn't a substitute for a qualified professional.
- **Subscription terms stated plainly:**
  - the free first scan;
  - weekly, monthly or annual billing;
  - the trial and when charging starts;
  - automatic renewal;
  - where to cancel;
  - what stays after cancelling.
- **Nothing gendered.** It reads the same to anyone.
- **Consistent with the privacy policy.** The copy doesn't say photos aren't
  used for AI training. The policy flags that claim as depending on a paid
  Gemini project, so leave it out of the listing until that's confirmed.
