## What GlowTrack is, and what it isn't

**GlowTrack gives cosmetic estimates of how your skin looks. That's all it
does.**

- It is **not** advice from a doctor or any other qualified professional.
- It does **not** identify, assess or rule out any skin problem.
- It is **not** a substitute for seeing a qualified professional. If you're
  worried about your skin, or something changes that concerns you, see a
  doctor. Don't rely on GlowTrack.
- The routine it suggests is a set of general cosmetic ideas. It isn't a
  set of instructions for you. Always follow a product's own instructions,
  and stop using anything that doesn't suit your skin.

## Your results are estimates

Each score is an estimate produced automatically by an AI model from a single
photo. The same face can score differently from one photo to the next. The
light, the camera, the angle, makeup and image quality all make a difference.
Scores are most useful for seeing a trend across several scans taken the same
way, not as a single exact measurement.

We make **no promise** that any score is correct. We make **no promise** that
using GlowTrack, or following a suggested routine, will change how your skin
looks.
